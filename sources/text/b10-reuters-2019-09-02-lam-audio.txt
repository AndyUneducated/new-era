In a recording obtained by Reuters, Carrie Lam told a private meeting that the havoc caused by her government's extradition bill was ‘unforgivable.’ Lam’s remarks provide the most transparent view yet of the Chinese leadership’s thinking on the political crisis rocking Hong Kong.

HONG KONG – Embattled Hong Kong leader Carrie Lam said she has caused “unforgivable” havoc by igniting the political crisis engulfing the city and would quit if she had a choice, according to an audio recording of remarks she made last week to a group of businesspeople.

“If I have a choice,” she said, speaking in English, “the first thing is to quit, having made a deep apology.”

“For a chief executive to have caused this huge havoc to Hong Kong is unforgivable,” she said.
