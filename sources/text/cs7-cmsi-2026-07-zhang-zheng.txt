Vice Admiral (VADM) Zhang Zheng (张峥) appears to have been promoted to the most senior billet in the People’s Liberation Army (PLA) Navy (PLAN). He seems to have taken up this responsibility as early as December 2025.

The apparent elevation of Zhang, coupled with PLAN Commander Admiral Hu Zhongming (胡中明)’s absence from publicly reported official PLAN activities since May 2025,[2] suggests that Hu may have been unceremoniously removed from his leadership post and succeeded by Zhang.[3]

Every year around the Spring Festival, the Central Military Commission (CMC) (中央军委) Chairman delivers greetings to PLA servicemembers, expressing appreciation for their hard work and encouraging them to train hard to build combat capabilities. The PLAN, like the other services and forces, does the same for its sailors, marines, and aviators posted throughout the People’s Republic of China (PRC), underway, and abroad. The PLAN Commander and Political Commissar deliver the Spring Festival remarks to the fleet (CMSI is unaware of any exemptions). In the PLAN’s 2026 Spring Festival greetings, a different officer occupied the seat where Admiral Hu Zhongming, a figure covered in CMSI Note 1,[4] once sat: VADM Zhang Zheng.[5] Since the PLA—like China’s entire Party-State-Military system—places great significance on protocol order and seating placement, this was the clearest sign that VADM Zhang had assumed the duties of leading the PLAN.

Figure 1: VADM Zhang Zheng (circled in red) wishes PLAN sailors, marines, and aviators a Happy Spring Festival in February 2026. Sitting to Zhang’s left is VADM Hu Yuhai (胡瑜海), PLAN Political Work Department Director (cirled in blue), who is likely Acting in the Capacity of PLAN Political Commissar.

Zhang likely assumed the PLAN’s most senior billet around December 2025. During the ceremony when CMC Chairman Xi Jinping promoted Yang Zhibin (杨志斌), Commander of the Eastern Theater Command and Han Shengyan (韩胜延), Commander of the Central Theater Command, to the rank of General, Zhang was present and wearing VADM shoulder boards.[37]

A persistent question remains concerning Zhang’s current role. As of this writing, official CCP/PRC/PLA media outlets have not explicitly confirmed Zhang’s selection to lead the PLAN. However, one source offers inferential evidence that Zhang is, or has served recently as, Chief of Staff (COS), PLAN. This suggests that he may be simultaneously performing the responsibilities of both billets.

It has become reasonably clear through news broadcasts that VADM Zhang Zheng has assumed the responsibility of leading the PLAN. He achieved this position by doing outstanding work in important billets amid the removal of numerous other PLAN leaders by disciplinary authorities. Zhang’s presence at key events also suggests that despite the positive and prominent coverage, VADM Cui Yuzhong (崔玉忠), a figure covered in CMSI Note 20,[42] did not assume a more senior, longstanding leadership role in the PLAN. It looks like the 57-year-old VADM Zhang, with abundant cutting-edge experience throughout the fleet and abroad, has a bright future ahead of him in the PLAN’s “era of three aircraft carriers.”
