Craig-y-Mor, an elegant 106-year-old home owned over the years by several prominent Sydney businessmen, was bought in 2008 by the son and daughter-in-law of a former senior member of the Chinese politburo for $32.4 million. In recent weeks, wrecking crews have embarked on a job that symbolises the international economic clout of the Chinese elite: razing the Australian mansion with stunning views of one of the world's most picturesque harbours.

Little is known about Zeng's personal or professional life. His father is Zeng Qinghong, the vice-president of China from 2003 to 2008 and a player in the leadership maneuvering over the past decade that led to the rise of President Xi Jinping.
