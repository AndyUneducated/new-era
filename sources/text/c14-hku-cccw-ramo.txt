Joshua Cooper RAMO 約書亞·雷默 Steering Committee MemberJoshua Cooper Ramo is currently the Chairman & Chief Executive Officer at Sornay LLC since 2021. He is also an Independent Director at FedEx Corp. since 2011. Additionally, he is a Member of the Council on Foreign Relations, Inc. and a Member-Global Leaders for Tomorrow at the Forum of Young Global Leaders. Previously, Mr. Ramo served as the Vice Chairman & Co-Chief Executive Officer at Kissinger Associates, Inc. from 2015 to 2020. He was an Independent Director at Starbucks Corp. from 2012 to 2023. He also worked as a Managing Partner at Jl Thornton & Co. LLC from 2003 to 2005. Mr. Ramo holds an undergraduate degree from The University of Chicago and a graduate degree from New York University.
